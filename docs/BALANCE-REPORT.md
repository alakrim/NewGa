# Bannerfall balance report

Date: 2026-10-26. Generated by `tools/balance.ts` (`npx tsx tools/balance.ts`). Seed 1. Measured against the working tree as found, including its uncommitted edits.

## Method

- Every measurement builds fresh `Game` instances with seed 1. No natural waves or jungle camps spawn during measurement.
- Dummy: a real aldric at the same level, so it keeps its real max HP, armor and magic resist. It has no regeneration, no attack damage, no attack range and no low-HP passive, so it never hits back. Its death is suppressed so damage keeps being counted. Its HP is reset at the start of every window, so percent-of-HP effects act on a realistic target. Missing-HP terms see the damage taken since that reset. The hero definition is cloned for this, so global hero data is untouched. Its armor and magic resist are real, so physical damage is multiplied by about 0.63 at level 6 and 0.54 at level 12, and magic damage by about 0.71 and 0.66. Raw numbers in the tables are after these reductions.
- Burst: hero at level 6 or 12, hero HP at 50% so self-healing is visible, no items. Q, W, E, R are cast at the dummy in order with 1.5s between casts (cooldowns and mana reset before each cast). Then 3s of basic attacks. Damage to the dummy is split into direct ability damage, on-hit procs (passives and empowered next hits, which run inside a basic hit) and basic-attack damage.
- Ally-targeted slots are cast on the hero itself, because no second friendly hero is placed. Bounce or ally-only effects (for example chain heals) are therefore under-measured.
- Summon hits (units spawned by the kit) are their own bucket. Hero basic attacks are suppressed in ability windows, so only the kit's own damage shows.
- Per-ability damage: each slot cast alone on a fresh game at L6 and L12, observed for 6s with basic attacks suppressed. This avoids mixing in zone ticks from earlier casts. A slot with damage effects that deals 0 is re-run at 2 units before it is flagged, because area and cone effects around the caster may not reach the 4-unit dummy.
- Sustained DPS: 10s of basic attacks at level 6, damage divided by 10.
- Wave clear: six melee lane minions (`MINIONS.melee`) in a ring of radius 1.2 about 6 units in front of a level-6 hero, with no dummy. The hero casts Q, W, E, R at the cluster (unit-targeted slots at the nearest minion) and auto-attacks the nearest live minion. Kills are counted at 4s. The run continues to a 60s cap to find the clear time.
- Survivability: effective HP at level 6 is `maxHp x (1 + armor/100)`, physical only. Self-sustain at level 6 is the sum of heal events on the hero (effective healing, from the sequence) plus shield amounts added to the hero during the burst sequence.
- Mobility: count of `dash` and `blink` effects in the kit (abilities and passive, walked recursively) and the sum of their distances.
- Outlier rule: a value more than 35% above or below its role average (role averages include the hero), a wave clear that does not finish within 60s, or an ability with damage effects (empower damage excluded) that dealt 0 damage in a successful cast.

## Engine and content notes

- bonusAd ignores level growth. src/shared/sim/game.ts evalNum() takes `bonusAd` as a fraction of attackDamage above baseStats.attackDamage, but recompute() sets hero baseStats from heroBase(level), which already includes growth. Probe: with no items, bonusAd(1) is 0.00 at L1, L6 and L12 and rises by 1.00 with a +10 AD buff. src/shared/types.ts says "items/buffs/levels above base", and docs/BALANCE.md budgets 0.7-1.2 bonusAd for physical abilities, so levels should count. Effect: physical abilities with bonusAd (for example Sable Q at 0.8) get no level scaling until the hero has items. Suggested fix: measure growth against heroBase(def, 1) rather than the live baseStats.
- Sable W (Cloak of Thorns): its damage is an empower on her next basic hit, so it reads 0 in an isolated cast and its damage appears in the burst as procs. Designed behaviour, not flagged.
- Ally-targeted slots (for example Nerissa Q, a chain heal) were cast on the hero itself, so bounce or ally-only effects are under-measured.
- Geometry, not a bug: Sable E at L6: 0 at 4 units, 32 at 2 units (area or cone around the caster; not flagged).
- Geometry, not a bug: Sable E at L12: 0 at 4 units, 49 at 2 units (area or cone around the caster; not flagged).
