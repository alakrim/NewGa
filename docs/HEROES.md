# Bannerfall: Hero Roster

Twenty champions fight over the Ley Banners of Vael. Any hero can be hired by either side: the Dawnguard (blue) and the Duskborn (red) pay in gold and ask no questions about old loyalties, so rivals, siblings and former teachers often find themselves on the same team, or on opposite sides, depending on the match.

**The setting in brief.** The Sundering shattered the Old Crown into the Ley Banners: relics that anchor magic to land. The Dawnguard Order (the knights of the Bastion of Aurel) and the Duskborn Pact (exiles, warlocks and raiders of Karth-Mor) war over them. Both bind the restless dead of the Sundering fields at their Bone Forges, raising the skeleton legions that march every lane.

Difficulty: **Easy** heroes have forgiving kits, **Medium** heroes reward good aim and timing, **Hard** heroes demand positioning, combos or micromanagement.

## Aldric, Shieldwarden of Aurel

*Tank | Easy | Dawnguard Order | Model: Knight*

> "A banner does not fall while someone is still standing in front of it."

Aldric Vane was nineteen when the Sundering split the Old Crown, and he spent that night on the walls of the Bastion of Aurel holding a shield taller than he was. The wall stood. Half the men beside him did not. Since then he has carried that shield into every siege and never once struck first, because the Dawnguard teach that a banner is held by those who refuse to move.

- **Passive, Oathbound 🛡️:** Every ability Aldric casts wraps him in a small shield. When he drops below 30% health he steels himself, gaining a surge of armor and magic resistance (long cooldown).
- **Q, Shield Rush 🐂:** Charges forward and slams into the first enemy in the way, damaging and briefly stunning them.
- **W, Hold the Line 🚩:** Raises a rallying aura. Nearby allies and Aldric gain a burst of armor and magic resistance, and Aldric gains a shield.
- **E, Provoke 📣:** Bellows a challenge. Nearby enemies take damage and are forced to attack Aldric for a moment while he hardens his guard.
- **R, Bastion Unbroken 🏰:** Plants himself and becomes unstoppable behind a huge shield, projecting a sanctuary that hardens every ally standing near him.

**Playstyle.** Cast Hold the Line just before a fight starts so the whole team gets the armor, then Shield Rush whoever is diving your carry. Save Provoke for the moment an assassin jumps in. Stay close to your team during Bastion Unbroken: the aura only helps people standing in it.

**Works well with:** Seraphine (her Aegis stacks on top of his aura and Intercession can save him), Skarra and Valen (he holds the front while they cut), Zhal (Static Field silences casters trying to burst him).

**Watch out for:** Rattle and Hrodgar (percent-health damage scales with his huge health pool), Ossian and Kaelen (long-range poke he cannot reach), Morvane (silences his rotation).

## Hrodgar, Ironhide of the Stonefang

*Tank | Easy | Ashen Wilds | Model: Barbarian*

> "You want to get past me? Then we will both be here a long while."

Hrodgar is chief of the Stonefang clan, and he earned the name Ironhide by surviving three winters of the Ashen Wilds with a spear through his shoulder and no one willing to pull it out. He fights in a cracked bronze cap, with a round shield and an axe he calls Stubborn. He does not understand why anyone would dodge a blow when a body can simply be made wide enough to absorb it.

- **Passive, Third Blow 🪨:** Every third basic attack deals bonus damage equal to a slice of the target's maximum health and heals Hrodgar. Devastating against bosses, camps and bulky heroes.
- **Q, Chain Hook ⚓:** Hurls a barbed chain. The first enemy it strikes is damaged and yanked straight to Hrodgar.
- **W, Earthshaker 🌋:** Stamps the ground, damaging nearby enemies and slowing them. Scales with his health.
- **E, Iron Will 💪:** Roars and shrugs off all crowd control for a moment, gaining a shield and a burst of speed to close the gap.
- **R, Rockfall Leap ⛰️:** Leaps to a point and crashes down like a falling boulder, damaging and launching every enemy in the landing zone.

**Playstyle.** Hook the most dangerous enemy and let your team collapse on them, then Earthshaker to keep them pinned. Iron Will makes you crowd-control proof, so use it to walk through a stun and land Rockfall Leap on the backline. Third Blow is strong when you trade blows with something big.

**Works well with:** Kaelen and Zhal (zones and meteors on a hooked target), Ysolde (a hooked enemy in a Blizzard cannot get out), Skarra (his sister; knockup then cleave).

**Watch out for:** Ossian and Merrow (they stay out of hook range and pin or slow him), Wren (blinks out of the combo), Dirge and Valen (their damage scales with missing health).

## Hallen, Marshal of the Unburied

*Tank | Easy | The Hollow | Model: Skeleton Warrior*

> "I buried my men once. I will not do it twice."

Marshal Hallen led the Dawnguard's third legion for thirty years and died in the ash fields on the first night of the Sundering with his shield still raised. He woke three days later among his own fallen soldiers, thinking, with the infinite calm of a man who has been ordering retreats for most of his life, that the line had held. It had not. But he had, and that was enough to make him rise.

- **Passive, Deathless 🧟:** The first time Hallen falls below 25% health he cannot be harmed for a moment and recovers a chunk of health. Long cooldown.
- **Q, Hollow Bash 🔨:** Smashes his shield into everything in front of him, damaging and shoving enemies back.
- **W, Muster the Fallen 🥁:** Calls two bone soldiers from the earth to fight beside him, and gains a shield.
- **E, Spectral Chains ⛓️:** Marks an area. After a heartbeat, ghostly chains burst out and pin every enemy there in place.
- **R, Dead March 🏴:** Sounds the old marching drum. A bone brute rises to guard him, and every ally nearby gets faster, hits faster and hardens for eight seconds.

**Playstyle.** Muster the Fallen early to push a wave with two bone soldiers, then use Spectral Chains to pin an enemy for your team. Deathless gives you one free mistake per fight, so dive once, survive the invulnerability and the fight is yours. Sound Dead March when your carries are within ten units.

**Works well with:** Zhal and Ossian (his old legion), Morvane (more bodies on the field), Tamsin and Merrow (he walls them in), Pell (healing the summons and Hallen at once).

**Watch out for:** Dirge and Valen (their damage scales with missing health, which punishes the Deathless window), Rattle (percent-health blasts), any kiting marksman who just walks away from a slow tank.

## Valen, The Sunblade

*Fighter | Medium | Dawnguard Order | Model: Knight*

> "Mercy is for those who still have something left to lose."

Valen Ashcroft is the Dawnguard's executioner, a title he hates and has never refused. When the Order must end a traitor, a fallen paladin or a Hollow that has gone feral, it sends the Sunblade: a two-handed greatsword forged from the melted bells of Aurel's first chapel. Valen does not gloat and does not hurry. He waits until the enemy is bleeding, then finishes it in a single stroke.

- **Passive, Sun's Judgment ☀️:** Every third basic attack burns with holy fire, dealing bonus magic damage that grows the more wounded the target is.
- **Q, Rising Slash ⚔️:** A wide upward cut that damages enemies in front of him and throws them into the air.
- **W, Lunge 🌅:** Leaps at an enemy and strikes them, then attacks much faster for a few seconds.
- **E, Riposte 🤺:** Raises his guard, taking far less damage. His next attack within a few seconds reaches farther and strikes back hard, slowing the target.
- **R, Final Verdict ⚖️:** Drops on a chosen enemy from above, dealing massive damage that grows with how wounded they already are. The shockwave hurts everyone nearby.

**Playstyle.** Open with Lunge to start attacking faster, then chain Rising Slash into your passive for the third-hit burn. Riposte before an enemy stun lands, and use the empowered strike to slow a runner. Keep Final Verdict for a target below half health: it scales with missing health.

**Works well with:** Aldric and Hrodgar (they hold the front line while he cuts), Seraphine (sustain plus Intercession), Nerissa (healing flows into his dives).

**Watch out for:** Merrow (root and roll away), Wren and Sable (stealth and kiting), Pell (shreds his armor, so he is easier to trade with).

## Skarra, The Rampant

*Fighter | Easy | Ashen Wilds | Model: Barbarian*

> "If it did not hurt a little, it was not a fight."

Skarra left the Stonefang clan at sixteen with a two-handed axe, a borrowed horse and a promise that her name would be sung louder than her brother's. The horse died in Mareth. The name did not take long: three winters in the Free City's arenas made her the Rampant, a woman who fights better the more it hurts and who has never been seen leaving a pit without a smile.

- **Passive, Bloodrage 👹:** Kills heal Skarra and put a spring in her step. When she falls below 30% health she flies into a rage, attacking much faster and stealing life with every blow.
- **Q, Rending Throw 🪓:** Hurls an axe that homes in on an enemy, wounding them and leaving a bleeding gash.
- **W, Cyclone 🌀:** Spins her axe in a whirling storm for over a second, shredding everything around her and picking up speed. Great against waves.
- **E, Savage Leap 🐺:** Vaults to a point and lands with a crushing blow, then fights with frenzied speed for a few seconds.
- **R, Blood Whirlwind 😡:** Erupts into a six-second frenzy: a storm of blades follows her, she heals from damage dealt, and she shrugs off crowd control at first.

**Playstyle.** Rending Throw to start a bleed, Savage Leap into melee for the attack-speed buff, and Cyclone while moving through a wave. Bloodrage can win a fight you were losing, so do not retreat the instant you drop under 30% health. Blood Whirlwind is a six-second window; do not waste it chasing.

**Works well with:** Nerissa and Pell (heals extend her rampage), Hrodgar (crowd control to keep enemies in the whirlwind), Kaelen (burst to finish what she wounded).

**Watch out for:** Hrodgar and Hallen (they out-trade her), Merrow and Ossian (pins and slows stop her gap-closers), Seraphine (shields and Intercession blunt the rampage).

## Kraghul, Chainbreaker of the Pits

*Fighter / Tank | Hard | Duskborn Pact | Model: Skeleton Warrior*

> "Chains are for holding the other man in place."

The pit masters of Karth-Mor say Kraghul died eleven times in the arena, and that each death earned the crowd a better show. Nobody knows what he was before the chains. The Duskborn Pact bought his contract, bound his bones with a Bone Forge rite, and pointed him at its enemies; he went willingly, because for the first time since waking in the sand he was being asked to fight someone who stayed down.

- **Passive, Crowd's Favorite 🏆:** Kraghul ignores a portion of enemy armor. Whenever he scores a kill, his Gladiator's Hook is instantly ready again.
- **Q, Gladiator's Hook 🪝:** Flings a chained hook. The first enemy struck is wounded and dragged all the way to Kraghul.
- **W, Spiked Chains 🔗:** Whips a ring of spiked chains around himself for a few seconds, cutting and slowing everything that gets close.
- **E, Whip Crack 💢:** Cracks his chain in a wide arc, damaging and slowing every enemy in front of him.
- **R, Last Man Standing 🥊:** Chains himself to an enemy and slams into them, rooting them in place for two seconds. Inside the duel Kraghul hits faster, hardens and steals life.

**Playstyle.** Gladiator's Hook is your whole game: drag the enemy carry in and drop Last Man Standing on them for a two-second root duel. Kills instantly reset the hook, so hook a wounded target for a chain of grabs. Spiked Chains gives armor while you brawl; use it just before engaging.

**Works well with:** Skarra (his rival; surprisingly good teammates), Morvane and Zhal (silence plus root plus hook is a lockdown), Pell (heals the brawl).

**Watch out for:** Wren and Seraphine (cleanse breaks the root; Intercession makes the duel pointless), Hallen and Aldric (they outlast him).

## Rattle, The Fuse-Keeper

*Fighter | Medium | The Hollow | Model: Skeleton Minion*

> "Every wall is just a door that has not met me yet."

Rattle was a sapper in the Free City's mining guild, a short-tempered dwarf-sized man with a love of black powder and a firm belief that every problem was a wall in disguise. The Sundering buried his crew under three hundred tons of Mareth granite. When he woke, the rock was gone, his flesh was gone, and the last thing he remembered was a spark. He decided on the spot that the spark had been his best idea yet.

- **Passive, Demolitionist 🔧:** Every basic attack deals bonus damage equal to a small percentage of the target's maximum health, which makes Rattle a boss and camp buster; his bombs and blasts also hit towers hard.
- **Q, Powder Keg 🛢️:** Lobs a keg to a point. After a short fuse it explodes, damaging enemies (more against high-health targets) and blasting them back.
- **W, Blast Jump 🚀:** Sets off a charge at his feet and rockets to a point, damaging and slowing enemies where he lands.
- **E, Sapper's Charge 🧨:** Braces behind a shield and arms his next attack with a satchel charge, which deals heavy bonus damage based on target max health and splashes nearby enemies.
- **R, Chain Reaction 💥:** Lights every fuse he has. After a short delay a giant blast tears through a huge area, dealing heavy damage (scaled by max health) and launching enemies. Rattle gains a shield while the fuse burns.

**Playstyle.** Powder Keg on a choke point or tower to push waves and bases, Sapper's Charge before a boss or camp fight. Blast Jump is both an escape and an engage. Chain Reaction takes 1.3 seconds, so bait with Blast Jump or fight in tight spaces where enemies cannot step out of the circle.

**Works well with:** Pell (his best friend; shields and heals while he blows things up), Hrodgar (crowd control to land the Keg), Hallen and Morvane (summons to walk waves into the tower).

**Watch out for:** Ossian and Kaelen (outrange the fuse), Wren and Sable (dodge the telegraphs), Aldric (armor and shield soak the physical blasts).

## Sable, Thorn of the Thornwood

*Assassin | Hard | Thornwood | Model: Rogue*

> "You were never going to see it coming. That was the whole point."

Sable Thorne was the best cutpurse the Thornwood Guild ever trained, and the first to rob the Dawnguard's own vault. The Sun-Seal ring she lifted from the Bastion of Aurel is worth more than a small town and does nothing but sit on her finger, glowing slightly whenever Valen Ashcroft is within a mile. She finds this hilarious. He does not.

- **Passive, Venomed Blades 🧪:** Basic attacks coat the target in nightshade, dealing magic damage over a few seconds.
- **Q, Throwing Knives 🔪:** Flicks a knife in a straight line that pierces through everything in its path, wounding and slowing them.
- **W, Cloak of Thorns 🌿:** Melts into the shadows and sprints away unseen. Her next attack from stealth strikes for bonus damage and silences the target.
- **E, Thorn Step 💨:** Slips a short distance in any direction, leaving a cloud of choking thorns that damages and slows enemies where she started.
- **R, Death Blossom 🌸:** Dashes onto an enemy and unleashes a flurry of blades, striking again and again while untouchable.

**Playstyle.** Cloak of Thorns, wait for the silence-opener on a mage or support, then Thorn Step out when they react. Venomed Blades tick after you retreat, so you can poke in and out. Death Blossom makes you invulnerable for a second: use it as an escape as often as an execution.

**Works well with:** Tamsin (old partner; she covers the line while Sable dives), Wren (her former apprentice, still fights in sync), Morvane and Zhal (silence plus backline dive).

**Watch out for:** Zone and area heroes (Kaelen, Ysolde, Pell) punish her short stays; Zhal's Static Field silences her mid-combo; Ossian punishes the entry; Aldric and Hallen are too tanky to burst.

## Wren, The Unseen Hand

*Assassin | Hard | Thornwood | Model: Rogue Hooded*

> "I was never hiding. You were just not looking."

Wren was nine when Sable Thorne found her sleeping in a bell tower and eleven when she picked her first lock. Fourteen years later she is the fastest blade in the Thornwood Guild and has not spoken to her teacher in two of them. The break was nothing dramatic. Wren simply realised that Sable had never once asked where she wanted to go, and that she herself had never wondered.

- **Passive, Ghost Step 👣:** Casting any ability gives Wren a short burst of speed, so she can chain blinks and kills without slowing down.
- **Q, Boomerang Blades 🪃:** Throws a spinning blade that cuts everything in its path, then returns to her hand, cutting everything on the way back too.
- **W, Shadow Blink 🌑:** Teleports to a point and slashes everyone around her on arrival, slowing them.
- **E, Shadowmeld 🕶️:** Shakes off every crowd control effect and fades from sight for a moment, moving faster while hidden.
- **R, Umbral Rush 🦅:** Blinks forward three times in a row, carving up everything at each landing. She picks the line; nothing in it stays standing.

**Playstyle.** Boomerang Blades hit twice, so throw them through a wave and a hero together. Shadow Blink in, damage and slow, then Shadowmeld to cleanse any root and disappear. Umbral Rush is a line, not a target, so cast it through the whole enemy team.

**Works well with:** Sable (her teacher), Merrow (a quiet friendship), Kraghul and Hrodgar (they hold enemies in place while she cleans up).

**Watch out for:** Zhal and Pell (silence field and armor shred), Kaelen (Backdraft peels her), Aldric (taunt, then she cannot dive freely).

## Dirge, The Quiet Hand

*Assassin | Hard | The Hollow | Model: Skeleton Rogue*

> "The Quiet Hand takes what it is owed. (Thornwood saying)"

Dirge ran the Thornwood Guild for twenty years and sold half its best students to the highest bidder. When the Sundering came he was in a cellar in Mareth counting coin, and the dead found him there. The Bone Forge raised him as the one thing it could not teach: silence. He has not spoken a word since waking. He moves without the click of bone, and when he strikes there is no sound at all.

- **Passive, Reaper's Momentum 🥀:** Killing a unit instantly refreshes Shade Slip and hides Dirge from sight for a moment, so a chain of kills never has to stop.
- **Q, Hamstring 🦵:** Dashes at an enemy and cuts their legs, damaging and slowing them.
- **W, Rend 🩸:** Rakes both blades through a wide arc in front of him. The more wounded an enemy is, the more it bleeds.
- **E, Shade Slip 🌫️:** Vanishes and reappears a short distance away, unseen for a moment and moving fast.
- **R, Guillotine 🗡️:** Falls on an enemy for a brutal strike that deals far more damage the less health they have left.

**Playstyle.** Hamstring a fleeing target and Rend them: Rend hurts more the lower their health. Every kill refreshes Shade Slip and hides you briefly, so a wave of weak targets becomes a ladder. Guillotine is a finisher, not an opener: wait for a target under half health.

**Works well with:** Morvane and Zhal (silence and slow set up the kill), Sable (his old student, though she wants him dead), Kraghul (hooked targets are easy to Hamstring).

**Watch out for:** Ossian (hunting him), Aldric and Hallen (taunt and walls), Seraphine and Nerissa (heals deny the execute).

## Ysolde, Frostweaver of the Ivory Spire

*Mage | Medium | Ivory Spire | Model: Mage*

> "Patience is just cold with better manners."

Ysolde Vauntry became the youngest Archmage of the Ivory Spire by proving that cold is not the absence of heat but a kind of patience. At the Sundering she was the only mage in the college who did not panic: she froze the burning library solid, saved three hundred volumes and was promptly reprimanded for ruining the shelves. She has not apologised.

- **Passive, Rime Touch ❄️:** Her attacks chill enemies a little, and every third hit deals bonus magic damage and slows the target heavily.
- **Q, Glacial Lance 🧊:** Launches a lance of ice that pierces through every enemy in its path, damaging and slowing them.
- **W, Frost Step ⛸️:** Blinks a short distance. A burst of frost chills enemies she left behind, and she gains a small shield on arrival.
- **E, Blizzard 🌨️:** Summons a freezing storm over a target area. Enemies inside take steady damage and are slowed.
- **R, Absolute Zero 🥶:** Marks a huge circle. After a moment it flash-freezes everyone inside, dealing heavy damage and stunning them, then leaves a lingering patch of deep cold.

**Playstyle.** Glacial Lance down a lane to chill everything, Blizzard on a choke point or banner, and Frost Step when someone gets close. Your third attack slows hard, so auto-attack between spells. Absolute Zero is a telegraphed circle; use it on enemies who are already rooted, hooked or tangled with your tank.

**Works well with:** Hrodgar and Kraghul (hooks into Blizzard), Aldric (the front line your zones need), Nerissa (her friend and former pupil).

**Watch out for:** Sable and Wren (they blink onto her), Kaelen (out-damages her), Zhal (silence field shuts her down), Ossian (outranges her).

## Kaelen, Emberfell the Exile

*Mage | Easy | Duskborn Pact | Model: Mage*

> "The east wing was already condemned. I merely helped."

Kaelen Emberfell was the most gifted pyromancer the Ivory Spire had produced in a century, and the only student ever expelled for setting the east wing alight to see how it looked from the lawn. He argued, with some justice, that the east wing was already condemned. The Spire disagreed, and an exile is a very easy person for the Duskborn Pact to recruit.

- **Passive, Ember Brand 🕯️:** Casting any ability sets his next basic attack ablaze, adding bonus magic damage.
- **Q, Fireball 🔥:** Hurls a ball of fire that damages the first enemy it strikes and then explodes, burning everything around the impact.
- **W, Meteor Strike ☄️:** Calls a meteor on a target area. After a short delay it crashes down, dealing heavy damage and leaving the ground burning.
- **E, Backdraft 🎆:** Vents a blast of flame in front of him, damaging enemies and throwing them back, while he gains a short burst of speed to flee.
- **R, Skyfall 🌠:** Calls down a colossal meteor anywhere in a huge range. After a long warning it explodes for massive damage and leaves a lake of fire behind.

**Playstyle.** Every spell empowers your next basic attack, so weave auto-attacks between casts. Meteor Strike is slow but strong: throw it on banners and choke points where enemies must stand. Backdraft is your panic button. Skyfall reaches nearly anywhere; use it to punish a recall, a banner capture or an enemy base push.

**Works well with:** Hrodgar and Kraghul (hooked enemies cannot dodge meteors), Aldric (taunt, then burn), Ysolde (a frozen enemy is a dead enemy, even if she hates it).

**Watch out for:** Wren and Sable (they blink in), Ossian (outranges and out-snipes), Seraphine and Nerissa (heal through the burn).

## Morvane, Boneweaver of the Hollow

*Mage | Hard | The Hollow | Model: Skeleton Mage*

> "The dead are not gone. They are merely between jobs."

Master Orlan Morvane spent forty years at the Ivory Spire cataloguing how magic dies. He was the first to notice that the Sundering fields were not dead but sleeping, and the last to be allowed to study them. He went anyway. When the Spire's wardens finally found his body in the ash, it was sitting upright with a journal in its lap, still writing: his notes grew calmer the longer he stayed dead, and the penmanship improved.

- **Passive, Harvest of Souls 💀:** Whenever Morvane gets a kill, even on a minion, a bone soldier claws its way up from the ground to fight for him.
- **Q, Soul Spear 👻:** Throws a screaming spectral skull that pierces everything in a line, dealing magic damage.
- **W, Raise Dead 🪦:** Raises two skeletal archers who fight for him for a short time.
- **E, Hex of Rot 🕸️:** Curses an enemy: they cannot cast spells for a moment, are slowed, and rot from the inside over several seconds.
- **R, Legion of the Hollow ⚰️:** Tears open the earth. A shockwave slows nearby enemies and a hulking bone brute and two wraiths rise to fight for him for eighteen seconds.

**Playstyle.** Kills raise skeletons even on minions, so farm waves with Soul Spear and let your soldiers fight for you. Raise Dead before a fight puts two archers behind your frontline. Hex of Rot is a silence plus a slow: use it on an assassin who is diving. Legion of the Hollow is both a zone-clearer and an army; use it for sieges.

**Works well with:** Hallen (more bodies), Pell (hates him, but heals his summons all the same), Kraghul and Hrodgar (hooks put enemies into his Soul Spear lines).

**Watch out for:** Sable and Wren (kill him before the army matters), Kaelen (area damage kills archers), Rattle (blasts clear summons).

## Zhal, Stormskull

*Mage | Medium | The Hollow | Model: Skeleton Mage*

> "Hear that? That is the sky agreeing with me."

Chaplain Zhal served Marshal Hallen's third legion as its priest of storms, a post that exists only in the Dawnguard's most northern chapels, where lightning is the closest thing to an answered prayer. He was struck by lightning at the moment of the Sundering. It did not kill him. What did was the Sundering itself, a heartbeat later, but the lightning stayed. His skull crackles when he is nervous, which is often, and there is a faint hum around his bones that dogs find unbearable.

- **Passive, Static Charge 🔋:** Every third basic attack releases a lightning arc that jumps to nearby enemies.
- **Q, Chain Lightning 🌩️:** Strikes an enemy with lightning that then jumps to up to three other nearby enemies, hitting each for the same damage.
- **W, Static Field 🔇:** Charges the ground. Enemies inside take small shocks and cannot cast spells for as long as they stay in the field.
- **E, Storm Surge 🏃:** Turns into a bolt and dashes in a line, shocking and slowing everyone he passes through, then attacks faster for a few seconds.
- **R, Stormcall 🌪️:** Summons a raging thunderstorm over a huge area. A first strike stuns everyone, and for five seconds lightning hammers everyone inside and slows them.

**Playstyle.** Static Field is a mage lockdown: put it on a banner or choke point so casters cannot spell inside. Chain Lightning bounces best into a minion wave next to a hero. Storm Surge dashes through a crowd and gives attack speed, so use it to start an auto-attack chain.

**Works well with:** Hallen and Ossian (old legion friends), Aldric (silence on the enemies chasing him), Kraghul (silence and pull).

**Watch out for:** Sable and Wren (dive him), Ossian and Merrow (outrange), Seraphine (cleanse and shield).

## Tamsin, Quickfingers of Mareth

*Marksman | Easy | Free City of Mareth | Model: Rogue*

> "Gold is just courage you can carry."

Tamsin Quickfingers will fight for whoever pays, and she will tell you so before you hire her. Born in the lanes of the Free City of Mareth, she has sold her crossbow to the Dawnguard, the Pact, three merchant princes and a travelling circus, and has switched sides in the middle of a battle only twice. Both times, she insists, the other side had better terms.

- **Passive, Lucky Shot 🪙:** Gains a little critical chance every level, and every third bolt hits with a bonus shot of extra damage.
- **Q, Piercing Bolt 🏹:** Fires a heavy bolt that punches through every enemy in a long line. Great for clearing waves.
- **W, Roll Out 🎲:** Dives backwards out of danger and shoots faster for a few seconds.
- **E, Caltrops 📌:** Scatters a patch of caltrops that shreds and slows any enemy that walks across them for a few seconds.
- **R, Bolt Barrage 🎯:** Empties her quiver in a rapid volley, firing eight piercing bolts down a line in under a second.

**Playstyle.** Piercing Bolt through the lane clears waves while poking heroes. Roll Out is both a dodge and an attack-speed boost, so use it to reposition and keep shooting. Caltrops cover a retreat. Bolt Barrage is a line, not a target: wait until enemies are lined up.

**Works well with:** Aldric and Hallen (protection), Nerissa and Seraphine (healers love a marksman who stays alive), Sable (old partner; protects the backline from assassins).

**Watch out for:** Wren, Sable and Dirge (assassins who reach her), Kaelen and Ysolde (burst her at range), Ossian (outranges her).

## Merrow, Harpooner of the Grey Tide

*Marksman | Medium | Tidewardens | Model: Rogue Hooded*

> "The sea gives back everything. Eventually."

Merrow grew up on the cold coast where the Tidewardens listen to the sea and hunt what the sea throws up. His sister Nerissa took the druid's path and learned the songs. Merrow took the harpoon and learned the dark water, where he fought something no Warden will name aloud and came home with a scar the shape of a hook. He still sleeps with his boots on.

- **Passive, Sea Legs 🐚:** Each harpoon he lands gives Merrow a short burst of speed, which lets him kite melee enemies all day.
- **Q, Pinning Harpoon 🔱:** Hurls a harpoon that damages the first enemy hit and pins them in place for a second.
- **W, Riptide Roll 🐬:** Rolls forward on a rush of water, then splashes outwards, hitting and shoving away anything close.
- **E, Sharpened Line 🎣:** Tightens his lines, gaining a lot of attack speed and a little extra reach for a few seconds.
- **R, Kraken Call 🦑:** Calls a tentacle from the deep. After a short delay it erupts, hurling every enemy in the area into the air, and leaves the ground churning and slowing.

**Playstyle.** Pinning Harpoon roots a target, so combine with your team's burst or your own autos. Your attacks give speed, so kite melee heroes endlessly. Sharpened Line extends your range and attack speed; use it before a fight. Kraken Call locks an area for team-fight chaos.

**Works well with:** Nerissa (his sister; heals and speed), Aldric and Hrodgar (front line), Ysolde (slows and zone control).

## Ossian, Longshot of the Unburied

*Marksman | Medium | The Hollow | Model: Skeleton Rogue*

> "One shot. I only ever needed one."

Ossian was Marshal Hallen's best scout, a quiet man who could place a bolt through a keyhole at two hundred paces and who vanished on the first night of the Sundering, hired to be killed. He woke in the ash fields with a crossbow bolt in his skull and no memory of the shooter, only a name: Dirge. The Hollow did not choose him. He chose them, because at last there was a place where no one would ask him to stop looking.

- **Passive, Marked for the Grave 👁️:** After casting an ability, his next basic attack reaches farther and hits for bonus damage while slowing the target.

**Playstyle.** After any spell, your next attack reaches farther and slows, so use Hollow Snare and then auto-attack at max range. Steady Aim lengthens your reach and damage for five seconds, so pop it before a poke war or a siege. Judgment from the Grave reaches half the map: use it to finish someone fleeing or to snipe a banner capturer.

**Works well with:** Hallen and Zhal (his old legion), Aldric (walls), Pell and Seraphine (healing for a stationary shooter).

## Seraphine, Lightwarden of the Dawn

Seraphine Vane took her vows as a Lightwarden the morning after the Sundering, when she found her brother Aldric asleep on his feet and covered in other men's blood. The Lightwardens do not heal with potions; they channel what little of the Old Crown's glow still lingers in Aurel's stones. It is a gift that drains her, and she has never once refused to give it.

## Pell, Gravetender of the Fields

## Schema wishes

Mechanics the current data schema could not express, so the kits above work around them.

1. **Structure damage.** `targetMaxHp` and `targetMissingHp` scalings are ignored against towers and fortresses by the sim (they only help against heroes, minions and monsters), so Rattle and Hrodgar are boss and camp busters, not tower killers. A `vsStructures` multiplier on `damage`, or a `Scaling` field like `structure`, would let siege heroes shine.
2. **Self-penalty buffs.** A buff with a negative value is treated as a debuff and only lands on enemies, so a "sniper stance" with a move-speed penalty (Ossian's Steady Aim) cannot be written. Allowing negative `pct` on allies/self would fix it.
3. **Conditional effects.** There is no "if the target dies / is stunned / is stealthed" branch. Dirge's Guillotine wants "refresh on kill", Sable's opener wants "bonus damage from stealth", Valen's ultimate wants "execute under 15%". A `when` condition on `Effect` would unlock a lot of kits.
4. **Scattered or random effects.** `repeat` repeats the same effect at the same location. A barrage that falls at random points inside an area (a true Skyfall) needs a `scatter` or `random` option.
5. **Per-rank counts, durations and radii.** Only `Scaling.base` is per rank. Summon `count` and `duration`, `radius`, `bounces` and `delay` cannot grow with rank.
6. **Stacking buffs.** Re-applying the same buff id refreshes it; there are no stacks, so "gain 5% attack speed per hit up to 5 times" is impossible.
7. **Reveal and terrain.** No effect reveals stealth, and no effect creates walls or terrain. Both would help counter-play for the three stealth heroes and give Hallen's Spectral Chains a true wall.
8. **Choosing a summon spawn point.** Summons spawn at the caster, or around `ctx.point` when `count > 1`. A point or rank-scaled spawn position would help Pell's Mass Grave and Morvane's Legion.
